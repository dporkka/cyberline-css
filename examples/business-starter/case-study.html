THE CONTEXT

Too many handoffs. Too little visibility.

Imagine a regional service company coordinating work through spreadsheets, email threads, and disconnected dashboards.

Staff repeatedly reconstruct job status instead of acting on one clear view of the workflow.

THE APPROACH

Design around the work, not the software.

01

Map the journey

Identify real user goals, transitions, and places where context is lost.

02

Consolidate the view

Give the next action, owner, and relevant history a consistent home.

03

Test the handoffs

Verify key workflows with representative tasks, errors, and narrow screens.

WHAT SUCCESS WOULD LOOK LIKE
